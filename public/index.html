About

CoCanvas is a free, instant real-time collaborative whiteboard, live coding workspace, and multi-page visual canvas.

Switch between Fixed Page Mode for clean multi-page presentations and Playground Mode for infinite drawing! Sketch with 9 MS Paint brushes, code in real-time, brainstorm with sticky notes, and present seamlessly in Host Mode.

Enjoy continuous voice typing with live translation, page-isolated collaborators, laser pointers, stickers, chat, and 2x HD Retina export. Have fun collaborating!

🔒 Privacy Policy

1. In-Memory Real-Time Canvas

CoCanvas is built with privacy-first engineering. Canvas drawings, sticky notes, codes, and live chat reside in transient server memory during your active collaborative session. We do not store permanent logs or sell your drawings.

2. Local Storage Only

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to save your chosen nickname, avatar, and light/dark theme preference across page reloads.

3. No 3rd-Party Tracking Cookies

CoCanvas does not deploy cross-site advertising cookies, surveillance beacons, or 3rd-party tracking scripts. Your canvas is your private whiteboard.

4. Security & Room Isolation

Each room is completely isolated with a unique 6-character room code. Host controls provide anti-abuse kick protection to keep your sessions secure.

📜 Terms of Service

1. Free & Open Usage
